Need help with Stationery Merge: Paper Shop? Contact the developer at To.approx@gmail.com.

Common questions

How does energy return? Energy refills automatically over time. Completing orders and optional rewarded videos can also add energy.

How do I restore Remove Ads? Open the menu, choose Settings, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I reset the game? Open Settings and use Start Over. This removes locally stored progress.
