Paper Corner: Cozy Shop — Support

Your little stationery shop of everyday joys.

How to play

  1. Tap a delivery box to put its goods on the shelves.
  2. Read the customer's order in the speech bubble, then tap the matching shelves to add goods to the basket.
  3. Tap “− remove” on a basket item to return one piece. “Return extra items” puts all excess goods back while keeping the requested quantities.
  4. Hand over the exact order to earn coins. Income, supply costs and decoration expenses are recorded separately.
  5. Use your earned profit for decorations. Tap the cat for a hint about the next step.

Language, music and game mode

Choose a language on the first launch, or tap the globe later. English, German, Japanese, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Russian are available. Use the music button to turn the original background melody on or off. Choose relaxed play or 90-second timed orders. The mobile game uses portrait orientation.

Saved progress

No game account or chat login is needed. Each local player profile has its own shop and daily records. Progress stays on the device. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove your saves; purchases are handled separately by the store.

Remove ads and restore purchases

The ad-free upgrade is a one-time purchase with a US base price of $1.99. Your store shows the final local price before confirmation. Open the ad-free window and choose “Restore purchases” using the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase. A pending or cancelled purchase does not unlock the feature. A store connection is needed to buy or restore.

Family settings

On first use, each player may enter a birth year or leave it blank. Only an age category is kept on this device. Children and players of unknown age receive no ads. A grown-up can open Family settings in Help to change the category. Purchases, adult profiles and external links require a parental check.
