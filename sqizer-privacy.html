SQIZER · FITNESS & WELLNESS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Local information

Sqizer does not require an account. Workout history, completed sessions, progress, onboarding answers and preferences are stored on your device. The current version does not upload workout history to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and permissions

Sqizer includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Free access may display banner advertisements and a full-screen advertisement at the end of a completed workout, when advertising is configured and privacy requirements permit. Advertisements are configured for free access and are requested only when privacy requirements and consent status permit. Sqizer does not use your camera or microphone or record your screen. With your permission, Sqizer schedules local daily reminders at your chosen time. Reminder settings stay on your device and can be disabled in Settings.

Advertising data and choices

When advertising is active, Google may process IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, crash logs and performance information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Sqizer requests non-personalized advertisements and does not send workout history, onboarding answers, symptoms or exercise-program information to AdMob. Non-personalized ads may still process technical data. Consent is requested where required before ad requests; available choices can be reopened in Settings under Advertising privacy choices. The current integration does not request Apple tracking permission. Google handles advertising data under its privacy policies.

Premium and advertising

Confirmed active Premium access disables ad requests and removes app advertisements. Selecting a subscription or using a purchase preview does not establish paid access. Data already processed by advertising providers before Premium activation remains subject to their retention practices. Sqizer includes no separate developer analytics or Firebase service and does not sell personal information.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes purchases and may process account, transaction and technical information under its own privacy policy. Sqizer can receive product and purchase-status information needed to restore access. Sqizer does not receive payment-card details. Subscription availability depends on store configuration. When configured, RevenueCat verifies store purchases and manages Premium access using an anonymous app user identifier, product and transaction information, purchase receipts or tokens, subscription status and related technical information. No name, email, workout history or health information is sent to RevenueCat by Sqizer. Advertising identifier collection and optional diagnostics are disabled in the purchase SDK. RevenueCat processes purchase data on our behalf; its privacy information is available at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Contact support about purchase-data privacy requests; uninstalling the app does not delete transaction records held by stores or purchase providers.

Storage and deletion

Local data remains until you clear app data or delete the app. Device backups or restoration may preserve app data under your operating-system settings. Review your backup settings when deleting information. Sqizer currently has no cloud account or cross-device workout-history sync. Platform storage protections apply, but no storage method is completely secure.

Support requests

If you email support, the information you choose to send, your email address and correspondence are used to answer your request. Avoid sending sensitive health information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; contact us about deletion or other privacy rights available under your local law.

Children and changes

Sqizer is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request information from children. We will update this policy and its effective date when data practices change.
