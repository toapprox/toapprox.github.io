Privacy Policy

Stationery Merge: Paper Shop
Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stationery Merge: Paper Shop (the “Game”) handles data. The Game does not require registration or a user account.

1. Local game data

Progress, coins, energy, language and sound settings are stored locally on the device. The developer does not receive this game-progress data. You can delete it by clearing the app data in Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), interactions with the Game and ads, diagnostic information, advertising identifiers, App Set ID and other device or account identifiers. These data may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance.

Advertising data may be shared with Google and its advertising partners. The developer does not sell personal data. Google’s processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and its partner sites information.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent before production ads are requested. Available choices can be reviewed through “Ad privacy choices” inside the Game. Ads may be personalized, limited or non-personalized depending on region, consent and device settings.

4. Google Play purchases

The optional Remove Ads feature is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment and provides the Game with the purchase entitlement needed to unlock or restore the feature. The developer does not receive payment-card details.

5. Permissions

Internet and network-state access are used for advertising, consent choices and purchases. The Game does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or access to personal files.

6. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The Game does not knowingly collect names, email addresses or account details from children.

7. Retention, security and deletion

Local progress remains until app data is cleared or the Game is removed. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Advertising-data retention and deletion are governed by Google and its partners. Users can reset or delete the Android advertising identifier in Android settings and can contact the developer with privacy questions.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the Game, third-party services or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page.

Реклама Google AdMob

Google Mobile Ads SDK может автоматически собирать и передавать IP-адрес (для определения приблизительного местоположения), взаимодействия с Игрой и рекламой, диагностические данные, рекламный идентификатор, App Set ID и другие идентификаторы устройства или аккаунта. Цели: реклама и маркетинг, аналитика, предотвращение мошенничества, безопасность и соблюдение требований.

В необходимых регионах Google UMP запрашивает согласие до загрузки рекламы. Доступные варианты можно изменить через пункт «Настройки рекламы и согласия» в Игре.

Покупки, разрешения и удаление

Разовая покупка отключения рекламы обрабатывается Google Play Billing; разработчик не получает данные банковской карты. Игра использует интернет и состояние сети, но не запрашивает точное местоположение, контакты, камеру, микрофон или личные файлы. Локальные данные удаляются через настройки Android или при удалении Игры.
