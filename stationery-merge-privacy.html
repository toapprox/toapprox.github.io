Privacy Policy for Stationery Merge: Paper Shop

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Stationery Merge is a mobile game that does not require an account or registration.

Local game data

Game progress, coins, energy, language and settings are stored locally on the device. The developer does not receive this game data. It can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its data.

Google AdMob

The game uses Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads may process coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash information, performance data and other diagnostics to provide and measure advertising, prevent fraud, maintain security and perform analytics. Depending on consent and device settings, a device identifier may be used for advertising tracking.

Google processes data under its Privacy Policy and partner-sites terms.

Consent and tracking choices

Where required, the game uses Google User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent. On iOS, Apple App Tracking Transparency is requested before tracking access. Refusing tracking does not block gameplay. Available advertising privacy choices can be reopened from the game's Settings screen.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads feature is a one-time purchase processed by Apple StoreKit on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android. The relevant store handles payment details and only reports purchase entitlement to the game. The developer does not receive payment-card details.

Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game is not submitted to Apple's Kids category.

Retention and sharing

Local progress remains until the app data is cleared or the game is deleted. The developer does not sell personal information. Advertising data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners according to user consent, device settings and their policies.
