Paper Corner: Cozy Shop — Privacy Policy

Updated October 13, 2026

Paper Corner: Cozy Shop is developed by Anastasiia Dmytriieva. This policy explains how the mobile game handles information.

Your shop stays on your device

The game stores player names, language, orders, inventory, coins, decorations and daily income and expense records locally on your device. The game does not require an online account and does not upload this progress to a developer server. Local player profiles are separate game saves, not online accounts.

Advertising

The game initializes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform only for eligible adult profiles, to display advertisements and manage applicable privacy choices. It does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads for children or players of unknown age. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share an IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, advertising interactions, product interactions, crash logs and performance or diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. For eligible adult profiles, the game requests non-personalized ads with a general-audience content filter, subject to applicable consent choices. The game does not request permission for cross-app tracking. The game does not request GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Google's data handling is described in its Privacy Policy and information about partner services. Applicable advertising choices can be reviewed using “Ad privacy” in the game's ad-free purchase window when that option is required in your region. Device advertising controls are also available in Android or iOS settings. The shop itself can be played offline; ads require a connection.

Optional purchase

A one-time, non-consumable purchase removes advertising. Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment. The game verifies the store entitlement to enable the ad-free feature; it does not receive your full payment card details. Purchases can be restored with the same store account. Once the game has verified the ad-free entitlement, it stops displaying ads.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives the information you include, such as your email address, message and any screenshots you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Your controls and deletion

You can choose another language or player profile in the game. Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove local game saves. Backups managed by your operating system or its cloud service may remain subject to those services' settings. Advertising data is handled by Google under its policies. Contact support to request deletion of personal information you have sent in a support message. There is no developer-hosted game account to delete.

Family play and age information

Each local player profile may enter a birth year on first use. The year is used on the device to select an age category and is then discarded; only the age category is saved locally. Leaving the field blank selects the restricted category. Children, younger players and users whose age is unknown receive no third-party advertising. Switching to an adult profile or opening purchases, advertising privacy choices and external support links requires a parental gate. A parental gate is an access check, not consent to collect a child’s information. The developer does not receive birth years or player profiles.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support message, please contact the developer to request review and removal. Parents should supervise store purchases.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The current policy and its update date are available on this page.
