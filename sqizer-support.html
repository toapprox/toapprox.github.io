Support

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Get in touch

For help with Sqizer, email sqizerapp@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or sensitive medical details.

Restore or manage purchases

Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the account used for the original purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. If products are unavailable, check your store connection and try again later.

Workout history

Workout history and preferences are stored locally. There is currently no Sqizer account or cloud history sync. Removing app data can remove your history; operating-system backups may preserve a copy.

Exercise questions

Sqizer gives general exercise guidance. For pain, pelvic symptoms, difficulty relaxing or advice about an individual condition, contact a qualified healthcare professional rather than app support.

Advertising privacy and Premium

Free access may display non-personalized AdMob advertisements when enabled. Settings offers Advertising privacy choices when required by the consent system. Confirmed active Premium access, including an eligible restored Lifetime purchase, removes advertisements. If ads remain after a purchase, restore with the original store account and contact support.
